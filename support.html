Support

Questions, suggestions or a problem? Email salahaura@pm.me, or use Settings › Contact Us in the app. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and the app version (Settings › About SalahAura).

Common questions

The prayer times look different from my mosque's.
Check the calculation method and Asr setting in Settings › Prayer Settings. To match your mosque exactly, use Settings › Prayer Settings › Prayer Time Adjustments to move any time earlier or later.

I'm not getting notifications.
In the app, open the Tools tab › Prayer Time Notifications and Adhan and turn on Prayer Notifications, and make sure notifications are allowed for SalahAura in your iPhone's Settings app. Open SalahAura at least once every week or so to keep alerts scheduled ahead.

How do I log a prayer from the widget?
Tap ⊕ next to a prayer once its time has started: once for on time, again for late, again for missed. Your log appears in the app the next time it opens.

How is the streak counted?
Each fard prayer prayed on time adds one. A prayer logged late or missed ends the streak, and the next on-time prayer starts a new one.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?
Your iPhone's iCloud Backup includes it. You can also use Settings › Your Data › Export Data to Backup File, then Restore Data from Backup File on the new phone and choose what to restore: prayer history, qada and settings. Your location isn't in the file; SalahAura works it out again.

Why don't I hear the adhan?
Choose it per prayer in the Tools tab › Prayer Time Notifications and Adhan › Adhan. It plays the first 30 seconds with the prayer's notification, and only when your iPhone's ringer is on (not on silent) and notifications are allowed for SalahAura. Tap the notification to hear the full adhan in the app.

How do I make up prayers I missed in the past (qada)?
Turn on Settings › What to Track › Track Missed Prayers (Qada), then open the Tools tab › Make Up Missed Prayers and add the prayers you missed. Nothing has to be exact, and you can change it any time. Each time you make one up, tap that prayer on the Qada card on the Prayers tab, or on a Qada widget. You can set a daily or weekly goal to see when you'd finish.

What is the Qada lantern?
The lantern on the Qada card fills as you make up prayers and is full when you reach your goal. If you turn on Qada Lantern Uses Fuel (in Make Up Missed Prayers), it slowly uses its fuel and stays lit while you keep a steady pace; an optional reminder comes a few hours before it would go out. The Tools tab › Qada Lantern Guide shows how it works.

What are excused days?
Turn on Settings › What to Track › Track Excused Days to mark days when you're not permitted to pray. Choose the prayer they began with, and later the one they ended with, from the Prayers tab. Excused prayers aren't added to qada, aren't counted in your stats and don't reset your streak. Like everything you log, they stay on your iPhone.
