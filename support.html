Support

Questions, suggestions or a problem? Email salahaura@pm.me. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and the app version (Settings › Other › About SalahAura).

Common questions

The prayer times look different from my mosque's.
Check the calculation method and Asr setting in Settings › Prayer Time Calculation. To match your mosque exactly, use Settings › Advanced Settings › Prayer Time Adjustments to move any time earlier or later.

I'm not getting notifications.
Turn on Settings › Prayer Notifications in the app, and make sure notifications are allowed for SalahAura in your iPhone's Settings app. Open SalahAura at least once every week or so to keep alerts scheduled ahead.

How do I log a prayer from the widget?
Tap ⊕ next to a prayer once its time has started: once for on time, again for late, again for missed. Your log appears in the app the next time it opens.

How is the streak counted?
Each fard prayer prayed on time adds one. A prayer logged late or missed ends the streak, and the next on-time prayer starts a new one.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?
Your iPhone's iCloud Backup includes it. You can also use Settings › Advanced Settings › Your Data › Export Data to Backup File, then Restore Data from Backup File on the new phone.
