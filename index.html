SalahAura
Helping you prepare for the first question on the Day of Judgment

SalahAura is an iPhone app that helps you pray every prayer on time, and make up the ones you've missed.

  • Log each fard prayer as prayed on time, late or missed, and keep track of qada
  • Accurate prayer times calculated on your iPhone, with Fajr's and Isha's end times
  • Your on-time streak, a dashboard, widgets, Qibla and the Hijri calendar
  • English, Arabic and Urdu

Private by design: no ads, no account, and no personal information collected.
