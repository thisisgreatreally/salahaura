Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

SalahAura does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We don't know who you are.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your prayer log, qada, streaks and settings are stored only on your iPhone.
  • If you use iCloud Backup, your iPhone's own backup includes the app's data, under Apple's privacy policy. We can't access it.
  • Backup files you export are saved only where you choose.

Location

  • Your location is used on your iPhone to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. It is never sent to us.
  • To show your city's name, the app uses Apple's location services. City search and the map use Apple Maps. These are covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Notifications and widgets

  • Prayer-time notifications are scheduled on your iPhone; nothing is sent from a server.
  • Widgets read the same data on your iPhone.

Contacting us and Send to Support

  • Contact Us and Send to Support open an email in your own Mail app. Nothing is sent unless you send it, and you see everything first.
  • SalahAura keeps a small activity log on your iPhone (app version, settings, prayers logged and from where, notification scheduling and errors) to help fix problems. It never includes your location, city, trip details or anything you typed, and it stays on your iPhone.
  • Send to Support attaches that log and a diagnostics file (app and iOS version, iPhone model, settings, and your country and time zone, but not your location). You can preview both before sending. Your prayer history is attached only if you turn it on.
  • Emails and files you send reach our Proton Mail inbox. We use them only to answer you and fix the problem, never share or sell them, and delete them once the problem is solved. To have yours deleted sooner, just ask.

Children

SalahAura doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Deleting SalahAura removes all of its data from your iPhone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
